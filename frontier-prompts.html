# Time view: the edge of what is known

Copy a question into Orbforma. These are research questions, not predictions presented as discoveries. Begin with the [SOI announcement](https://schmidtocean.org/the-doldrums-fracture-zone-is-anything-but-dull/) and [MBARI expedition](https://www.mbari.org/expedition/the-doldrums-fracture-zone/), then check subsequent primary research.

Test the proposed hybrid plumbing

Create an ORB about the Doldrums Atlantic hydrothermal vent discovery from the May–June 2026 expedition. Separate observed vent fields and measured samples from the proposed hybrid plumbing interpretation. Compare possible heat sources, fluid pathways and rock–water reactions without assuming the interpretation is confirmed. What fluid chemistry, mineralogy, temperature measurements, mapping and subsurface evidence would distinguish competing explanations? Organize established evidence, open questions, conditional predictions and feasible next tests. Cite primary sources and their dates, identify limitations, and explain what result would change the interpretation.

How widespread is circulation?

Create an ORB asking how representative the newly reported Doldrums vent fields are of circulation along Atlantic transform faults. Distinguish finding two fields from estimating how many exist. Examine search coverage, instrument sensitivity, sampling bias and the difference between not detected and absent. Compare localized circulation with more widespread systems as hypotheses, without inventing probabilities. What systematic surveys and repeated observations would discriminate between them? Include sources, uncertainties, maps where supported, and implications for ocean–crust chemical exchange only where evidence permits.

How does vent life change over time?

Create an ORB about how biological communities at newly discovered Atlantic hydrothermal vents might change over time. Distinguish a first expedition snapshot from a measured time series. Explore how fluid chemistry, energy availability, dispersal and disturbance could influence microbes and animals. Separate general vent ecology from Doldrums findings. Propose repeat sampling and environmental measurements that could test these explanations. State alternative outcomes and the evidence each would require. Cite primary research; do not invent a site-specific species inventory or trend.
